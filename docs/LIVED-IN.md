# LIVED-IN — making the house a home, not a model house

Adam's notes, 2026-11-14. Textures alone don't cure "model house" —
empty rooms with nice floors still look like a showroom. What sells
lived-in is stuff, softness, and signs of the specific people.

## The law of the house

**Any flat surface is an insult to their sensibilities, and gets
filled with junk.** (Adam's words, about his people — it is also
this family's truth.) Every counter, table, dresser top, and shelf
in the game gets its clutter. An empty surface is a bug.

## The five levers, in payoff order

1. **Clutter on surfaces** — the big one. Books, mail, mugs, fruit,
   remotes: real houses have stuff on every horizontal plane.
2. **Soft things** — curtains, bedding, towels, throw rugs. Hard
   surfaces everywhere = showroom; fabric breaks it.
3. **Wall life** — picture frames, posters in the bedrooms, photos
   and magnets on the fridge, things IN the curio cabinet. Rooms
   start belonging to specific people.
4. **Light** — (batch 2, in flight) warm lamp pools against cool
   window spill at night. Model houses are lit evenly; real ones
   aren't.
5. **Wear, lightly** — a rug worn thin in the hall path, small
   scuffs. Overdone grime tips into haunted-house cosplay and
   fights the comedy.

## Adam's specifics (from the real house)

- **Clothes on the floors.** Seemingly random heaps in bedrooms,
  the bathroom, by the laundry. Built as low rumpled GEOMETRY
  (a few offset boxes per pile), not a texture or normal map —
  the flat-shaded style would swallow a texture whole.
- **The curio cabinet** holds the upright display dishes nobody
  is allowed to use (the good china), teacups, a vase. There are
  more things in there Adam can't recall yet — stub left open;
  he'll remember when he sees it stocked.
- **Potted plants hanging in various windows.**
- **One annoyingly biggish potted plant on the dining room
  table**, amongst the rest of the clutter.
- **A coffee table in the living room** — forgotten until now.
  Goes before the 3-seat couch.
- **Two big comfy chairs** against the wall in the little space
  to the right of the stairs as one comes down.
- **Laundry baskets** — full ones, near the clothes heaps.
